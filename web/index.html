About

IPFS handles the files. 13xfile handles the experience.

The app manages uploads, local encryption, replication, sharing, and peer presence without pretending a traditional cloud bucket is decentralized.

Content addressed

Files are referenced by CID, so links describe content rather than a specific storage server.

Encrypted when needed

Sensitive files can be encrypted locally before being placed on the network.

Public when intended

Public files can be opened through share.13xfile.app and public IPFS gateways.

How it works

Simple on the surface, decentralized underneath.

01

Upload

Choose Public or Encrypted in the desktop app.

02

Store on IPFS

13xfile adds and replicates the content across participating peers.

03

Share or publish

Create a direct share link, or optionally publish public metadata to the 13xfile feed.

13xfile Feed

Discovery is separate from storage.

The official feed can search and sort public metadata quickly, while the underlying metadata and files remain addressable through IPFS.
